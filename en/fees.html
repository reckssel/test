§ 1 Amount of fees

  1. The association charges annual fees as shown in the following table.
  2. For supporting and corporate members, the amount shown is a minimum fee. A higher fee is welcome.

§ 2 Due date

  1. The annual fee is due on 31 January of each year for the current financial year.
  2. When joining during the year, the fee is due one month after admission.

Back to top

§ 3 Method of payment

  1. The fee is preferably collected by SEPA direct debit. For this, the member grants a SEPA direct debit mandate. The association announces the collection at least five days in advance (pre-notification).
  2. Alternatively, the fee may be transferred to the association’s account. Please state your name and “Membership fee [year]” as the payment reference.
  3. Costs of returned direct debits for which the member is responsible (e.g. insufficient funds, changed bank details) are borne by the member.

Back to top

§ 4 Joining during the year

Members joining in the first half of the year pay the full annual fee; members joining from 1 July pay half the annual fee.

Back to top

§ 5 Leaving

  1. Resignation is governed by § 6 of the statutes (three months’ notice to the end of the financial year).
  2. Fees already paid are not refunded on resignation or expulsion.

Back to top

§ 6 Reductions and exemptions

  1. The reduced fee applies to school pupils, apprentices, students, pensioners and recipients of social benefits. Proof must be submitted to the board every year without being asked.
  2. In cases of particular hardship, the board may, on application, defer, further reduce or waive the fee. Applications are treated confidentially.

Back to top

§ 7 Late payment

  1. If a fee has not been paid two months after it falls due, the association sends a reminder in text form. After a second unsuccessful reminder, the board may expel the member in accordance with § 6 of the statutes.
  2. No reminder fees are charged. [Placeholder – to be decided by the association]

§ 8 Entry into force

These membership fee rules come into force by resolution of the general meeting on [TT.MM.JJJJ].
